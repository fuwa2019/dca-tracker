## Reading

- The account is already generating serious reach: 777.3K impressions in 7 days.
- The main weakness is engagement quality, not raw traffic. A 0.6% engagement rate is low for a comment-driven plan.
- Replies are strong relative to reposts and bookmarks. That suggests the current behavior is good at conversational interaction, but not yet producing enough "worth saving / worth sharing" posts.
- Reposts are extremely low at 8. A pure reply-farming approach will not fix this; the hourly plan needs more posts with transferable insight, checklists, strong takes, or useful comparisons.
- Verified followers at 1.8K / 2.2K means the account is close to the visible 2.2K reference count shown in the UI. The plan should protect account quality and avoid spam-like tactics.

## Strategy Adjustment

Keep the comment-trigger requirement, but split hourly posts into two modes:

1. Reply-first posts: ask for experience, binary choices, ranking, or correction.
2. Save/share-first posts: compact tool tips, checklists, contrarian lessons, repeatable workflows, and source-backed market explainers.

Daily mix should be adjusted inside the existing 24-post plan:

- 12 reply-first posts.
- 8 save/share-first posts.
- 4 personality / light posts.

The long post should not just summarize the highest-reply topic. Prefer topics that combine replies + bookmarks + profile visits.

## Immediate Content Hypotheses

- Codex/Mac/Windows posts should include mini-checklists, because tool tips are more bookmarkable than general opinions.
- Investment posts should explain "how I would think about this signal" rather than ask only "what do you think", because the latter gets replies but not saves.
- Light/funny posts can pull replies, but should not dominate the day because they may not convert profile visits into followers.
- News quick takes must include a personal framework or source link; otherwise they look like aggregation and may hurt monetization quality.

## Next Measurement Targets

For the next 7 days, track these manually or via CSV:

- Reply rate by trigger type.
- Bookmark rate by category.
- Repost rate by category.
- Profile-visit-to-follow conversion.
- Long-post contribution to follows.
- Whether yellow-risk trend posts underperform after verification delays.

## Operational Rule Update

Every hourly post should now be tagged:

- `reply_first`
- `save_share_first`
- `personality`

Do not publish 24 posts that all optimize for replies. The account needs a visible reason to follow, save, and share.
