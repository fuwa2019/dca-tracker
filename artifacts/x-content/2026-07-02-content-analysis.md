## Diagnosis

- Current reach is driven by identity/community posts, screenshots, and light social jokes. These posts create replies but almost no reposts.
- Tool and investing posts are not failing on reply quality; they are failing at distribution. The subject matter is suitable, but the packaging is too niche or too late in the funnel.
- `#蓝V互关` worked for raw exposure, but it is risky to overuse for creator monetization quality. Future content should reduce mutual-follow framing and move toward useful, original, experience-based posts.
- The account voice that works: casual, self-aware, slightly funny, not polished like a newsletter.
- The missing layer: save/share-first utility. Useful posts need a stronger hook, a compact list, and a comment trigger that invites real experience.

## Strategy Update

1. Keep 4-6 light/personality posts daily, because they warm the account and fit the current audience.
2. Convert tool/investing posts into public experiments, mini-checklists, and "which one would you choose" formats.
3. Use hot topics as the opening hook, but make the body about your personal workflow: Codex, Mac, Windows, Schwab, IBKR, BOXX, SGOV, TradingView, Claude, or content operations.
4. Do not make every post a question. Mix:
   - reply-first: experience request, binary choice, ranking.
   - save-share-first: checklist, setup path, mistake list, source-backed explainer.
   - personality: light status, self-deprecating observations, daily creator notes.

## Immediate Rules For Hourly Posts

- If a post is about tools, include a reproducible scenario: what task, what setting, what result, what uncertainty.
- If a post is about investing, avoid "buy/sell" framing; use "how I classify this signal".
- If a post is about X growth, avoid "互关/互赞/评论领取" mechanics. Ask for experience instead.
- If a post references news or platform changes, mark it yellow until the original source is checked.
